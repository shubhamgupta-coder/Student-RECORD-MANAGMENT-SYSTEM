Place the pre-trained model files in this folder before running or packaging the app:

1. classifyWaste.h5
   - 9-class Keras/VGG16 waste classification model (input 224x224x3, output 9 classes):
     Batteries, Clothes, E-waste, Glass, Light Blubs, Metal, Organic, Paper, Plastic

2. teaching_feature_bank.npz
   - Required only when using "Improve AI Model" on the Teach AI / Train Model tab.
   - Must contain arrays:
     - train_features (float32, shape [N, feature_dim])
     - train_labels (int32, shape [N])
     - validation_features (float32, shape [M, feature_dim])
     - validation_labels (int32, shape [M])
